    How the ship works

    • Speed = how much of the crew is taking part: cards, votes and owned action items.
    • Sea = how the sprint felt. Rocks make it rough, wind calms it.
    • Rocks clear when someone owns an action for them.
    • A darker rock is a problem that came back.
    • Neither speed nor sea is a score. Ships are never compared between teams.
